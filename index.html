Where it helps

Processes that eat hours every week

If someone on your team spends their day copying information between systems, reading documents, or chasing follow-ups, that work can usually be automated.

Order intake

Today Orders arrive by email, PDF and WhatsApp, and someone types each one into the ERP.

Automated The agent reads the order, creates it in the ERP, and asks a person only when something is missing or unclear.

Customer support triage

Today Every ticket is read, categorised and routed by hand before anyone starts on it.

Automated Tickets are sorted, the customer's account is pulled from the CRM, and a draft reply is ready from your own docs.

Recurring reports

Today Every Monday, numbers are exported from four systems and pasted into a spreadsheet.

Automated A pipeline collects the data on schedule, builds the report, and flags anything unusual.

Keeping systems in sync

Today CRM, billing and ERP disagree, and someone reconciles them by hand.

Automated Changes flow between systems automatically, and conflicts are surfaced instead of silently overwritten.

Document review

Today Contracts, forms and compliance files are checked line by line against a checklist.

Automated The agent checks each document against your rules and highlights exactly what needs a human eye.

Follow-ups and chasing

Today Missing documents, unpaid invoices and pending approvals are tracked in someone's head.

Automated The agent tracks what's outstanding, sends the reminders, and updates the record when a reply comes in.

What we actually build

Three parts, built for your business

Every automation we deliver is made of the same three building blocks. You don't need to know the technical terms, but here is what's under the hood.

01

An agent shaped around your process

We don't drop in a generic bot. We write the agent's instructions, decide which tools it may use, encode your business rules and exceptions, and define the exact points where it stops and asks a person.

Technical term: a custom agent harness

02

Secure connections to your systems

The agent gets controlled access to the systems it needs — reading only what it should, and writing only where it's allowed. This works with modern cloud tools as well as older or in-house systems.

Technical term: MCP servers and custom integrations

03

Pipelines that run it day to day

The automation starts on its own — when an email arrives, a file is uploaded, or every morning at 7. Failures are retried, every action is logged, and you can always see what happened and why.

Technical term: event-driven data pipelines

How a project runs

Start with one process. Get it right. Then expand.

  1. 01

    Map the process

    We sit with the people who do the work today and write down every step, exception and system involved.

  2. 02

    Build and connect

    We build the agent, connect it to your systems, and set up the pipeline that runs it — tested on your real data.

  3. 03

    Run it alongside your team

    It starts in review mode: the agent prepares the work, a person approves it. We tune it on real cases until it's reliable.

  4. 04

    Hand over and maintain

    Once it's trusted, it runs on its own. We monitor it, keep it updated as your systems change, and move to the next process.
